PolicyHub — build 2026-10-07b
Opportunities by entity, and an investor passing on a deal

1. OPPORTUNITIES BY OWNER ENTITY
The same entity picker the dashboard and the policies grid carry is now
on Opportunities, at the top right. Tick one entity, several, or All.

It is the same selection, not a copy of it: choose LCG1 on the policies
grid and Opportunities opens on LCG1 too, and the other way round. The
subheading says "LCG1 only" while it is on, and an empty filtered list
says "Nothing open for LCG1 — choose All entities to see the whole book"
rather than reading as an empty book.

It narrows and never widens. A manager who names an entity that is not
his gets an empty list, not a look at it. Investors are not offered it
and are not affected by it.

2. AN INVESTOR PASSING ON A DEAL
Investors had two answers — ask for a piece, or say nothing — and the
desk could not tell "has not looked" from "looked and is not
interested". Now there is a third.

On the deal, under the request box: "Not one for you? Pass on this
deal". A reason is offered and never required — a short list of common
ones (the return, the wait, the size, the carrier, not investing just
now) and a box for their own words.

  · For the investor, the deal leaves their open list and moves to a
    "You passed on" heading at the bottom; the menu badge stops counting
    it. The deal page says they passed and what they said, and offers
    "I have changed my mind" while it is still open — which puts them
    back to undecided.
  · For the desk, the Shared with card's last column is now "Their
    answer": a percentage, "Passed" with the date and reason, or
    "nothing yet". The difference between the last two is whom to
    telephone.
  · A pass and a live request cannot both stand. An investor with a
    request in is asked to withdraw it first; quietly withdrawing it as
    a side effect would release a slice the desk may be about to confirm.
  · Nobody passes for them — staff are refused — and no investor is told
    whether anybody else passed.
