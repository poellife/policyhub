WHAT CHANGED SINCE 09-09a

  The death benefit and the benefit schedule are now ONE figure.

  They were two: the Death benefit field on the deal, and the first row
  of the premium schedule. Both claimed to be what the policy pays now,
  and nothing kept them in step — so retyping the schedule left the
  deal's field at the original, and the one-pager's headline, its "today"
  note and its "% of face" all read that stale field. The scenario
  columns were reading the schedule and were correct throughout, which is
  why the sheet looked half right.

  Now, on a deal flagged as having a changing benefit:

    edit the benefit on the DEAL      -> year one of the schedule follows
    edit the figures on the SCHEDULE  -> the deal's headline follows

  The later years do not move when "today" is edited. 2031's benefit is
  the carrier's own figure, not something to derive from this year's.
  The form says so.

  A schedule that starts AFTER the expected close says nothing about
  today, so the figure on the deal is left to cover that stretch alone.

  Level policies are untouched by all of it.
