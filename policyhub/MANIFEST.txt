WHAT THIS ADDS

  A CASE FILES LINK ON AN OPPORTUNITY. Edit a deal and paste the address
  of the folder that holds that client's documents — Dropbox, SharePoint,
  a drive, anything. It shows in the line under the deal's name as
  "Case files", opening in its own tab.

  WHO SEES IT

    The desk sees it.

    THE DOCTOR SEES IT. A case sent for medical review carries the
    folder through to his screen, under "The records", because he is
    being asked to read the file. He still sees no price, no death
    benefit and no rate of return.

    INVESTORS DO NOT. Not hidden on the page — the server does not send
    it to them at all. A shared folder is usually named after the
    client, and keeping that name off an investor's copy of the deal is
    the whole point of the initials on the sheet.

  IT TRAVELS ONTO THE POLICY when the deal is funded, so the folder that
  held the records while it was being priced is still linked once it is
  owned. Policies already had their own case-files link; this is the
  same field, filled in for you.

  Only http and https addresses are stored. A bare "dropbox.com/..." is
  read as https, which is what pasting one means. Who may actually open
  the folder is still decided by the folder's own sharing settings —
  pasting a link here grants nobody anything.

NOTHING TO RUN. Copy the files in and redeploy. The new column applies
itself on boot.
