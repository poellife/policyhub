NEW IN THIS ONE

  MEDICAL REVIEW IS ADMINISTRATORS ONLY. The Med Review tab and the
  panel on a case are gone for managers, editors and viewers — not
  hidden, absent: the server refuses the whole family to anybody but an
  administrator and the reviewing doctor, so a route added later is
  refused too. What a review produces, the life expectancy, is on the
  deal itself for everybody who needs it.

  MORE RECORDS, AFTER THE FIRST LOT. On a review, "Add a link" takes
  another address — a second folder, a hospital portal, the cardiology
  somebody had to send separately — with a word about what it is, and
  there is a drop area on the same card for files. Drop several at once;
  they go straight to that reviewer. Both appear on his screen under
  "More records", and either can be taken off again.

EVERYTHING ELSE IN THIS PACKAGE (25a–h)

  Drop a provider's report anywhere on the Life-expectancy reports card
  to file it against the case.

  Investors are quoted COMPOUNDED by default with a switch to simple —
  the fix for the sheet reading "simple interest" after you set
  compounded.

  The Med Review tab: every case sent, out or complete, with a way into
  the whole review.

  A four-line email to the doctor: initials, death benefit, and a link
  straight to the case.

  A case can go to several doctors; each answers on his own.

  His estimate lands as the INTERNAL LE beside the provider's report
  rather than over it. Investors never see it.

  His screen: an estimate, his reasoning, his call, one button. He sees
  the death benefit, never a price or a rate of return.

  A records link per review, which is the only folder he is shown.
  Withdraw takes a case off his screen; Delete removes the review.

NOTHING TO RUN. Copy the files in and redeploy. The new table and
columns apply themselves on boot.
