COMPOUNDED IS NOW THE DEFAULT EVERYWHERE

  The dashboard, the policy return tiles and the Maturities register all
  lead with the compounded figure — the IRR. A tick box in the page
  header, "Show simple interest", puts the simple column and the simple
  figure back beside it; unticked, every rate on the screen is
  compounded. The choice is remembered against the account.

  Where the screen prints "the other weighting reads X", that figure is
  now the other weighting of the SAME convention. It used to quote the
  simple one beside a compounded headline — two numbers differing for
  two different reasons, in one sentence.

THE MATURITY SHEET

  A tick box decides whether every transaction is listed. Off by
  default: the sheet stops at the totals — the purchase price, what the
  premiums came to and how many there were. Ticked, it lists the ledger
  as it moved.

  A second tick box decides whether the commission is named on it, also
  off by default.
