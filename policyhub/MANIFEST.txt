WHAT THIS ADDS

  A SEPARATE RECORDS LINK FOR THE DOCTOR. On a review, "Add the records
  link" takes a Dropbox address for the medical file — that folder and
  nothing else. It is the only folder the reviewing doctor is shown.

    The case folder on the deal is NOT shown to him and never was meant
    to be: it holds the illustration, the offer and the correspondence,
    which is the price. An earlier build did pass it through to his
    screen; that is corrected here.

    The link is also on the "Send for medical review" dialog, so it goes
    with the case the first time rather than being added afterwards.

    Sharing the folder with him in Dropbox is still a separate act. The
    link tells him where to look; it grants nothing.

  Withdrawing a request takes the link away with everything else — his
  screen loses the case entirely.

  A MED REVIEW TAB (this was build 2026-09-25a). Every case ever sent to
  a doctor, split into what is still out and what has come back, with
  the insured, the case, the reviewer, his estimate and his call.
  Opening one shows the whole review and the button that puts his
  estimate on the case.

  THE DOCTOR'S SCREEN is a life expectancy in months, a box for his
  reasoning, his call at the bottom, and one button reading "Review
  complete". The empty "nothing has been uploaded" and "no records
  summary" panels are gone — the page shows what is there.

  Uploading individual documents onto a review still works, for the odd
  single file. They are filed against the review, stay off the Documents
  tab, and no investor can reach them.

NOTHING TO RUN. Copy the files in and redeploy. The new columns apply
themselves on boot.
