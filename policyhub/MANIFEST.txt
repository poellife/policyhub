NEW IN THIS ONE

  A COMMISSION PER MATURITY. On the Maturities register, administrators
  get a Commission button on each row. It takes a percentage of the
  PROFIT AFTER COSTS — everything that came back, less the purchase
  price and every premium — and nothing is charged on a case that lost
  money. The dialog works the percentage out in dollars as you type it.

    Two new columns: what the commission comes to on that case, and the
    NET RETURN TO THE INVESTORS beside the gross one, simple and
    compounded. The net rate is solved again from the cash flows with
    the deduction taken off the final payment on its own date — not the
    gross rate with a percentage shaved off it, which is a different and
    wrong number.

    Leaving the box EMPTY means the owner entity's own rate stands.
    Entering 0 means nothing is charged on that case in particular.

    Administrators only. A manager sees neither column nor button, and
    an investor's register carries no trace of it — their figures are
    already net, as they always were.

  A MATURITY ONE-PAGER. A "Sheet" link on every row prints a single
  page: the case and its dates, what it cost (purchase price, premiums,
  other costs), what came back (death benefit, proceeds), the profit,
  the commission, the multiple, both rates, and the cash as it moved.

    Investors can print their own: their share, net, with the insured as
    initials and no mention of the commission.
