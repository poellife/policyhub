PolicyHub — build 2026-10-06c
Two things: deal notes, and writing the doctor's answer down for him

1. DEAL NOTES ON AN OPPORTUNITY — ADMINISTRATORS ONLY
A second notes box on every deal, beside the existing one.

  Notes for investors — what you already had. Every member of staff
                        reads it, and it belongs to the deal as shown.
  Deal notes          — yours. What the seller actually wants, where the
                        price came from, why the last offer was walked
                        away from.

Administrators only, in both directions. A manager is not sent the field
at all — not an empty string, absent — so there is nothing on the wire
for a screen to leak, and a write from anybody who is not an admin is
dropped before it reaches the database. It is on the deal's own screen
as its own card and in the edit form as its own box; for everybody else
neither exists. Not on the one-pager, not on an investor's screen.

2. ENTERING THE MEDICAL ANALYSIS YOURSELF
For a reviewing doctor who gives you his reading over the telephone
rather than typing it in.

On the review — on the deal's Medical review panel and on the review's
own screen — there is now "Enter his answer". It asks for the life
expectancy in months, whether that is a median or a mean, his reasoning
in his own words, what he suggests, and a line saying how the answer
came in ("By telephone, this afternoon"). It then behaves exactly as his
own return would: the review reads as back, and the estimate lands on
the case if the case has none, or waits beside it as the internal LE if
it already carries a number.

What it will not do is pretend he typed it:

  · Every screen showing the estimate also says who wrote it down, when,
    and how it came in — on the review, on the deal's panel, in the
    register.
  · Administrators only. A manager cannot do it, and nor can the doctor,
    who has a route of his own.
  · A review the doctor HAS written himself is refused rather than
    overwritten — his words stand.
  · If he later types his own, the office's handwriting comes off it and
    the review is simply his.
  · You can correct what you took down: the button becomes "Change what
    you took down" on a review the office recorded.
