Emailing the one-pager to an investor, PDF attached

WHERE IT IS
Open a deal, click One-pager, set the participation, and Build it. The
sheet opens with "Email it to an investor" above it:

  To        pick the investor. Those already shown the deal are listed
            first; anybody else can be chosen too, and sending shares the
            deal with them so the link in the email opens it. Their name
            goes at the top of the message when you pick them.
  Subject   prefilled, editable.
  Message   the covering note, prefilled from the deal's own figures and
            editable however you like.

  Send to investor    sends it, with the one-pager attached as a PDF.
  Send me a preview   sends the identical email to you only, marked
                      [Preview]. Nothing is shared and nobody else is
                      written to.

No download, no attaching. The PDF is drawn at the moment you press
Send, from the deal as it stands — the same document Download PDF gives.

HOW IT GOES OUT
From the firm's address (the same "Poel Capital Portal" sender as every
other message), with replies coming back to YOU — the investor who hits
Reply reaches whoever sent it. It goes immediately; the screen says
"Sent to …" or says what went wrong.

Every send is listed on the deal, staff only, under "One-pager emailed":
who, when, by whom, at what participation, and whether it was delivered
or bounced.

WHAT IT WILL NOT DO
  · Send an email that names the insured. The note is written in
    initials, but the box is editable, so the finished text is checked
    before anything leaves and refused with the word it found. Last
    names are caught however they are typed; first names as written on
    the record, so an insured called Will does not stop "we will".
    Writing to an investor who shares the insured's surname is fine as
    long as you use her full name; "Ms Coleman" alone is refused, because
    there is no telling whose name it is.
  · Send to anyone but an investor on file. You pick the person; the
    address is the one on their record. Managers can only pick investors
    in their own book.
  · Send a closed deal (it is on nobody's portal, so the link would lead
    nowhere). A preview of one is fine.

ALSO FIXED
The covering note now quotes the scenario investors are shown, when one
is chosen. It was quoting the deal's own figures while the PDF beside it
quoted the scenario's.

NEEDS EMAIL SET UP ON RENDER
Sending uses the same mail setup as the other messages (RESEND_API_KEY on
the Render service). If that is not set, the box says so and the Send
buttons stay off; Download PDF and "Copy the text instead" still work.
