INTEREST, WHICH IS THE ONE THAT WAS WRONG ON SCREEN

  An investor is now quoted COMPOUNDED by default and can switch to
  simple interest with the control in the page header — the same control
  the desk has always had. It used to be staff-only, so an investor saw
  whatever their account had never chosen, which was simple interest,
  while the office was quoting them an IRR. Their choice is remembered.

  The desk still defaults to simple interest, and accounts that have
  already chosen keep what they chose.

MEDICAL REVIEW

  THE EMAIL to a reviewing doctor is now four lines: what happened, the
  insured's initials, the death benefit, and a link straight to that
  case. No name, as before. The portal holds the link across the sign-in
  screen, so he lands on the case rather than on his queue.

  THE DEATH BENEFIT is now on his file, as asked. Still absent: the
  asking price and every rate of return.

  A CASE CAN GO TO MORE THAN ONE DOCTOR. Tick as many reviewers as you
  like; each gets their own review of the same life and neither can see
  the other's. Sending the same life to the same doctor twice is still
  refused.

  HIS ESTIMATE NO LONGER OVERWRITES THE REPORT. Where the case already
  carries a life expectancy, his lands beside it as the INTERNAL LE —
  visible on the deal and in the edit form, never sent to investors, and
  the deal goes on being priced off the provider's report. A case with
  no estimate at all still takes his automatically; there is nothing to
  preserve.

  A REVIEW CAN BE DELETED outright (administrators), which is different
  from Withdraw: withdraw takes the case back and keeps the record;
  delete removes the row and the papers with it.

LIFE-EXPECTANCY REPORTS

  "Attach a report…" on the panel takes a provider's PDF — 21st, Fasano,
  ITM — and files it against the case, with a note. It sits beside the
  reports this application ran, opens from there, and can be removed.
  It stays off the Documents tab, is behind the same grant as the runs,
  and no investor can reach it.
