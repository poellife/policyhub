The investors' folder

WHAT THIS ADDS
A second document link on every opportunity and every policy: an
investor folder, separate from the office's case folder.

  Case files       — the desk's working folder. Staff only. Investors
                     never see it, and neither does a reviewing doctor.
  Investor documents — the link handed to the people who own a piece of
                     the policy: policy documents and whatever of the
                     health picture you decide they should have.

On an investor's own screen there is only one folder and it is simply
called "Documents", because they have only the one.

The link set on an opportunity travels onto the policy when the deal is
funded, alongside the case folder and the issue date.

A reviewing doctor is sent neither folder — he sees only the records
folder attached to his own review.
