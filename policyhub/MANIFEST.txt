PolicyHub — build 2026-10-28a
Mortality Watch, as an administrators-only tab
(also contains everything in 2026-10-27a: emailing the one-pager)

WHAT IT IS
Your Mortality Watch tool, rebuilt inside the portal. Same scoring rules —
the original tool's own test cases pass point for point, so a 75 means the
same thing it did — but it watches the insureds already in the portal, so
there is no CSV to keep in sync.

  Who is watched   everybody insured under a policy in the active book
                   (In force, Grace, Pending) with no date of death on file.
                   A new policy is watched from the next sweep; a matured
                   one drops out by itself.
  Joint insureds   "Moe And Claire Engler" is one record in the portal and
                   two people to an obituary, so it is split into two. Neither
                   is given the shared date of birth — a notice whose birth
                   date conflicts is thrown out, so a wrong date is worse than
                   none — and both are flagged until you type each one in.

THE TAB (Mortality Watch, in the menu after Med Review)
  Waiting for a decision   each notice with its score, who it might be, the
                           policies at stake, what the notice says, a ✓/✗ row
                           showing why it scored what it did, and a link to it.
                           "It's them — confirm" / "Not them — dismiss".
  People watched           the details the scorer uses that the portal does
                           not hold: middle and maiden names, nicknames, city,
                           where they used to live, spouse and children. Edit
                           any row. The more filled in, the better a notice is
                           told from a namesake.
  Where it looks           the sources, switch each on or off, add your own:
                           a web search (optionally limited to certain sites),
                           an RSS feed, or a page listing recent notices.
                           Starts with the same four your config had. Shows
                           roughly how many paid searches a sweep will cost.
  Decided, Recent sweeps   history, with Reopen for a decision made by mistake.
  Run a sweep now          on demand; it shows progress while it runs.
  Check a name             a one-off lookup, scored, nothing saved.
  Upload a vendor file     a death-record vendor's CSV, scored against
                           everybody the moment it is uploaded. Column names
                           can be mapped if the vendor uses its own.

CONFIRMING DOES NOT RECORD A DEATH (as you chose)
Confirm marks the notice as theirs, stops searching for that person and
closes their other waiting notices. The insured's date of death is NOT
written — the policy stays in the active book until you enter it on the
insured once a certificate or the carrier confirms it. The tab says so on
every notice and in the confirm box.

DAILY SWEEP
Runs once a day after 6 AM Eastern, plus Run now. If anything is filed,
every administrator gets an email — initials, score, date and place only;
never the insured's name and never the notice's link (those usually spell
out the name). Admins can switch it off under their notification settings,
and the wording is editable in "What the messages say".

SETTINGS ON RENDER (Environment)
  BRAVE_API_KEY   needed for the web-search sources. Without it they find
                  nothing and the tab says so in red; listing pages, feeds
                  and vendor files still work. (GOOGLE_API_KEY or SERPAPI_KEY
                  instead, if you switch the sources to that provider.)
  MW_RUN_HOUR     optional, hour of the daily sweep, default 6
  MW_TIMEZONE     optional, default America/Detroit
  MW_SCHEDULE=off optional, turns the daily sweep off (Run now still works)
API keys stay on the server; the tab only ever shows whether one is set.

Cost: with the four starting sources it is about 9 searches per person per
sweep (5 + 2 + 2). The tab shows the figure for your book before you run.

The daily sweep needs the Render service awake at 6 AM — a paid instance
is; a free one that has gone to sleep will run it when it next wakes.

SAFETY
  · Administrators only, menu and server alike; managers and investors
    are refused every route.
  · The server will not fetch private or internal addresses (localhost,
    the cloud metadata address, private ranges) from a source or from a
    link on a listing page, and follows redirects only to public ones.
  · Searching sends insureds' names, with city or birth year, to the
    search provider — the same as the Python tool did.
