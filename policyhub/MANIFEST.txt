NEW IN THIS ONE

  DROP A REPORT ON THE CARD. A PDF dropped anywhere on the
  "Life-expectancy reports" panel is filed against the case at once,
  named after the file. The card outlines itself while a file is over
  it. "Attach a report…" still opens the dialog when the name or a note
  matters — and that dialog now takes a dropped file too, and fills the
  title in from the file name.

EVERYTHING ELSE IN THIS PACKAGE (from 25a–f)

  INTEREST: investors are quoted COMPOUNDED by default and can switch to
  simple with the control in the page header. That is the fix for the
  sheet reading "simple interest" after you had set compounded — the
  control was staff-only, so their account showed a default it had never
  been asked about. The desk still defaults to simple.

  MED REVIEW TAB: every case sent to a doctor, split into what is out
  and what has come back, with a way into the whole review.

  THE EMAIL to a doctor is four lines — what happened, the initials, the
  death benefit, and a link straight to that case.

  A CASE CAN GO TO SEVERAL DOCTORS. Each gets his own review and neither
  sees the other's.

  HIS ESTIMATE NO LONGER OVERWRITES THE PROVIDER'S REPORT — it lands
  beside it as the INTERNAL LE, which investors never see.

  HIS SCREEN is an estimate, his reasoning, his call, and one button
  reading "Review complete". He sees the death benefit; he still sees no
  price and no rate of return.

  A SEPARATE RECORDS LINK per review, which is the only folder he is
  shown. The deal's case folder is never sent to him.

  WITHDRAW takes a case off his screen entirely; DELETE removes the
  review and its papers from the register.

  ATTACHED PROVIDER REPORTS sit on the case beside the runs, off the
  Documents tab, behind the same grant, invisible to investors.

NOTHING TO RUN. Copy the files in and redeploy. The new columns apply
