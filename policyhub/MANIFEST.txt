THE ONE-PAGER IS GROSS, ALWAYS

  Every figure on the maturity sheet is now before commission: the
  profit, the multiple, and both rates. There is no tick box for it any
  more and no way for the commission to reach that page — one document
  with one meaning, whoever ends up holding it.

  The commission and the return AFTER it are read in the portal, on the
  Maturities register, by the administrators who set the rate: the
  Commission column and the "Net to investors" column beside it.

  The sheet still has its tick box for listing every transaction, off by
  default.

EVERYTHING ELSE, AS BEFORE

  COMPOUNDED IS THE DEFAULT on the dashboard, the policy return tiles
  and the Maturities register, with a "Show simple interest" box in the
  page header.

  THE REPORT WEIGHTING BUG is fixed: a document asked for equal-weighted
  now foots with the equal-weighted figure instead of the
  capital-weighted one.

  A COMMISSION PER MATURITY, administrators only — a percentage of the
  profit after costs, with the after-commission return solved from the
  cash flows rather than shaved off the rate.

  On a matured policy the policy page offers the One-pager instead of
  "Offer to investors".

  Medical review is administrators only, with its own tab, files and
  links for the reviewing doctor, the internal LE beside the provider's
  report, and a screen cut back to an estimate, his reasoning and one
  button.
