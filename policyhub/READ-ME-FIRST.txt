PolicyHub build 2026-11-11c
(Includes everything since 2026-11-01a.)

Upload into the policyhub folder of the repo:
  src/            -> policyhub/src
  public/         -> policyhub/public   (app.js, reports.js, irr.js, build.js, styles.css)
  public/vendor/  -> policyhub/public/vendor   <-- NEW FOLDER. Create "vendor" inside
                     public and upload html2canvas.min.js, jspdf.umd.min.js and the two
                     LICENSE files. Without it, Download PDF will not work.
  scripts/        -> policyhub/scripts  (tests, optional)

New since 2026-11-11a
- Reports -> Download PDF now saves the report exactly as it looks on screen
  (same tiles, tables, charts and type), on Letter pages, portrait or landscape
  as the report prints, broken between rows rather than through them. Every
  report on the Reports page works this way.
- CFO login: the entity menu lists only the CFO's own entities, even when
  someone else used the same browser tab before them, and choosing an entity
  now actually filters. The "Portfolio return" tile is gone from the CFO's
  dashboard (the compounding choice is on Realized).
