The database adds the two new fields itself on the next start.

New: High valuation and Low valuation per policy
- Open a policy: the Overview tab now starts with a "Valuation" card. Type a
  figure in High valuation or Low valuation and press Enter (or click away).
  It saves by itself; there is no Edit dialog. Blank a box to clear it.
- Reports -> Valuation report: two new columns, High valuation and Low
  valuation, with totals. It prints and downloads with them.
- Low cannot be above high. Staff only: investors and CFO logins do not see it.
- Optional: Policies -> Columns lets you switch the two on in the grid.
