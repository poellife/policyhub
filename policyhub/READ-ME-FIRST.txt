PolicyHub build 2026-11-08b: CFO login
(This zip also contains the compounding and multi-entity investor builds,
2026-11-04a and 2026-11-08a, so you can skip those zips and upload this one.)

Upload into the policyhub folder of the repo:
  src/      -> policyhub/src      (api.js, auth.js, schema.sql, opportunity-pdf.js,
                                   opportunity-email.js, maturity-pdf.js)
  public/   -> policyhub/public   (app.js, reports.js, irr.js, build.js, styles.css)
  scripts/  -> policyhub/scripts  (tests, optional)

No new environment variables. The database updates itself on the next start.

Setting up a CFO
  Settings -> Users -> New user -> Role "cfo" -> tick the owner entities -> Create.
  (An existing login: click it, change Role to cfo, pick the entities, Save.)

What the CFO sees
  Portfolio, Policies, each policy, Premiums and Realized: the investor's screens,
  across every policy owned by the chosen entities, at full-policy figures.
  With more than one entity they can filter by entity.
  Insureds show as initials. No investor names, no opportunities, documents,
  agreements or capital calls. Read-only: they cannot change anything.
  Removing an entity from their login takes effect immediately.
