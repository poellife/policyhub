POLICYHUB — THE APPLICATION FILES THAT CHANGED
==============================================

19 files. Everything the running deployment needs that is different from
what was there before. The test suites are in the other zip and are not
needed to run.

Drag the FOLDERS (public, src) into GitHub's uploader rather than the loose
files inside them, or you will end up with app.js in the repository root
instead of in public/ and the site will break in a way that looks like a
code problem.

  README.md                       what the application does and why
  render.yaml                     the deployment, including the mail settings
  .env.test.example               the example test configuration

  public/app.js                   the whole interface
  public/styles.css               the visual system
  public/policy-fields.js         the policy column catalogue
  public/irr.js                   simple interest, carried interest, pooling
  public/reports.js               the printable reports
  public/agreement-template.js    the operating agreement text
  public/charts.js                the charts

  src/api.js                      every route
  src/auth.js                     sign-in, sessions, the two clocks
  src/mail.js                     email: templates, queue, provider
  src/security.js                 sign-in locations, exports
  src/server.js                   startup, uploads, the mail worker
  src/schema.sql                  the database, applied on every start
  src/import.js                   the CSV and workbook importer
  src/agreement-pdf.js            the agreement PDF
  src/opportunity-analysis.js     opportunity scenarios
