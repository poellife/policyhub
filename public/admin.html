Notes: "Reset password" sets a new password and signs the family out of all devices — tell them the new one, and they can change nothing themselves (no email reset exists). "Deactivate" blocks email sign-in immediately; devices already signed in keep working until they sign out, and the raw family code still works (deactivation gates the account login, not the data). New signups show "awaiting approval" — they cannot sign in until you press Approve. Legacy code-only families (no account) appear in the family list below the accounts if they have no email attached. Homework is off for every family until you switch it on here — it lets that family photograph a mishnayos or chumash sheet and turn it into practice, and nobody else can even see the button.
🎙 Copy recordings between families — and into the standard voice
Recorded words belong to a family code, so a family that starts again — or a second test account — begins with nothing. This copies every word one family has recorded into another; the originals stay where they are.
To give every new family a voice from day one: record the whole word list on any account of your own, then copy that account into 🏠 The standard voice. Every family that has not recorded a word hears it, so the kriah games work the moment they sign up, and their own recordings replace it word by word as they make them.
Families without an account (code-only)

👪 Turn a family code into a real family
For a family that only ever had a code — an early family from before accounts, or a parent who set the app up on one device and never signed up. Type the code (or press the button beside one in the list above) and it becomes an ordinary family: same code, same child, not a minute of practice lost, and from then on they sign in by email like everyone else. 🎧 The standard voice
One set of readings — every kriah word and every Rashi letter — that any family who has not recorded that word yet will hear. It is applied on its own: every family already signed up, and every family that joins from now on, falls back to it word by word, so the kriah games, the 🔊 button and Read & Listen work from the moment they sign up. Nothing has to be pushed to anyone. When a parent records a word in their own voice, theirs takes over for their family — one word at a time, and only for them.
