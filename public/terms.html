Tell us promptly — through the feedback box in Parental Controls — if you think someone else has your code or password.

4. Your recordings

When you record the practice words, the Rashi letters, or the coach's lines in your own voice — and when your child records himself reading a line for you to listen to later — those recordings stay yours. We don't sell them, share them with other families, or use them to train anything. You give us permission only to store them and play them back inside the App for your own family, which is the whole point of them.

You can delete a recording at any time by re-recording over it, deleting it from the review list in Parental Controls, or asking us to remove your family's data. Your child's readings are kept only until you delete them, and we keep at most the 60 most recent per family.

5. Privacy — including children's data

What we collect

  • From the parent: your name, email address, and phone number if you give it, so we can set up and support your account.
  • About each child: the first name you type in, and their practice data — minutes practised, words read, accuracy and speed, chores and points, and which activities they completed.
  • Recordings you choose to make, as described above.
  • Basic technical data that any website receives, such as your IP address, used to keep the service running and to stop abuse.

What we don't do

  • We do not show advertising in the App, and we do not sell or rent anyone's data — ever, and especially not a child's.
  • We don't ask children for an email address, a photo, a location, or any way to contact them.
  • We don't use children's data for advertising or profiling.

Parental consent and control

Because the account is created and controlled by a parent or guardian, you are the one consenting to your child's information being collected, and you can see all of it inside the App at any time. Ask us through the App and we will send you a copy of your family's data, correct it, or delete the whole family permanently — usually within a few days.

The weekly progress note

If the weekly note is switched on, we send your child's practice statistics only — minutes, accuracy, which reading skills are strong or weak, and the Hebrew words being drilled — to an AI provider, which writes the short summary you see in the report and receive by email. Your child's name is never sent; it is filled in on our own server after the text comes back. No recordings and no contact details are ever sent. You can turn the whole thing off in Parental Controls → Account.

Who else sees it

Only the service providers we need to run the App: our hosting and database provider, and our email provider for account emails. They process data on our behalf and are not permitted to use it for anything else. If payment is enabled, card details go directly to our payment processor — we never see or store your card number.

We keep your family's data for as long as your account is open, and delete it when you ask us to or a reasonable period after the account is closed.
